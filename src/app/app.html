Biography

My story

Sana Burney is a master’s in Fine Art from UCLan — University of Central Lancashire, UK. She has taught for over 12 years and has been on the faculty of the Indus Valley School of Art & Architecture. Burney has presented research on art and design in Turkey, the USA and Pakistan, and has completed large-scale public art projects in Karachi and Larkana, including Karachi’s FTC Bridge Beautification.

In 2021 she participated in Jade Montserrat’s mural project at UCLan, Preston. Her practice revolves around colonialism, post-colonial societies, racialized inequality, belonging and not belonging, capitalism, and feminism. Hybrid identities and identity crises sit at the centre of the work, alongside sociology and psychoanalytic perspectives.

The practice originates in twentieth-century modern collage and moves through assemblage, found objects and contemporary experiment, including traditional printmaking taken into new methods.
